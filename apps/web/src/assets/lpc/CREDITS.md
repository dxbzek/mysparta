# Character art credits

The hunters in RANK ZERO are assembled from the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC)
universal character sprite set. Individual parts (bodies, heads, hair,
beards, eyes and clothing) were cropped to side-view combat strips and
recoloured at runtime; no artwork was redrawn.

Parts used: 75 LPC sheet definitions, assembled from the Universal
LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
(https://github.com/sanderfrenken/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
